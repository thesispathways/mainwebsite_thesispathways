Scholarships

ThesisPathways offers need-based scholarships for low-income students to make research mentorship accessible.

Eligibility: Students with demonstrated financial need. We consider factors such as household income, eligibility for free or reduced-price lunch, and other verified extenuating financial circumstances. Documentation may be requested to verify financial need.

  1. Submit the standard Student Application first.
  2. Then submit the Scholarship Application.

Both full and partial (half-price) scholarships are offered. Scholarships are limited, and acceptance to the program does not guarantee the award of a need-based scholarship.

Refund Policy

  • Before first session: Full refund, minus processing fee
  • After 1 session: 90% refund, minus processing fee
  • After 2–4 sessions: 50% refund, minus processing fee
  • After 5 sessions: No refund available
