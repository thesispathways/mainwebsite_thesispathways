Scholarships

At ThesisPathways, we are committed to expanding access to high-level research mentorship. We offer competitive merit-based to either fully or partially support outstanding and motivated students.

How to Apply to our Scholarships

After filling out the Student Application:

  1. To apply for our Merit-Based Scholarships, email thesispathways@gmail.com at least 30 days before the student application deadline to express your interest. Applicants will receive further instructions by email and will be asked to submit a resume and a personal essay.

Scholarships are limited. Admission to the program does not guarantee scholarship funding.

Refund Policy

  • Before first session: Full refund, minus processing fee
  • In certain cases, partial credit toward future sessions may be considered
