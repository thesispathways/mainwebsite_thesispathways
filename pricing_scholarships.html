Scholarships

At ThesisPathways, we are committed to expanding access to high-level research mentorship. We offer competitive merit-based and need-based scholarships to support outstanding and motivated students.

Merit-Based Scholarship

Awarded to students who demonstrate exceptional academic achievement, intellectual curiosity, leadership, or strong research potential.

  • Typically covers up to 50% of tuition
  • Based on academic performance and scholarship application essay
  • Highly competitive and limited

Need-Based Scholarship

Designed to ensure access for students with demonstrated financial need.

  • Full tuition support available
  • Based on verified financial need
  • Documentation may be requested

How to Apply to our Scholarships

  1. Submit the Student Application .
  2. Indicate your interest in scholarship consideration within the application. Interested candidates will be contacted with further instructions after submission.

Scholarships are limited. Admission to the program does not guarantee scholarship funding.

Refund Policy

  • Before first session: Full refund, minus processing fee
  • After 1 session: 90% refund, minus processing fee
  • After 2 sessions: 50% refund, minus processing fee
  • After 3 sessions: No refund available
