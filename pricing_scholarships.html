Scholarships

At ThesisPathways, we are committed to expanding access to high-level research mentorship. We offer competitive merit-based and need-based scholarships to support outstanding and motivated students.

How to Apply to our Scholarships

  1. Click here to apply for our Need-Based Scholarship.
  2. To apply for our Merit-Based Scholarships, email thesispathways@gmail.com to express your interest. Applicants will receive further instructions by email and will be asked to submit a resume and a personal essay.

Scholarships are limited. Admission to the program does not guarantee scholarship funding.
