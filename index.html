Why join ThesisPathways?

Colleges are getting more competitive than ever. High GPA and test scores alone aren’t enough anymore. Students need research experience, leadership skills, and excellent letters of recommendations to stand out.

  • Work one-on-one with mentors from top universities, including Ivy Leagues
  • Gain a competitive edge for college, graduate school, or medical school applications
  • Students at ThesisPathways are not research assistants; they get to be leaders of their own research projects
  • Explore your future career potential as a scientist, physician, engineer, economist, policy analyst, or scholar
  • Develop advanced research, academic writing, and critical thinking skills beyond the high school curriculum
  • Receive individualized mentorship and the opportunity to earn a personalized letter of recommendation
  • Get support through a structured program that guides you from idea to publication

What is Research?

Research is the process of asking a question and using evidence, data, and existing knowledge to investigate it. In ThesisPathways, students learn how to design a research question, analyze information, and write a structured academic paper with guidance from a PhD or MD mentor.
